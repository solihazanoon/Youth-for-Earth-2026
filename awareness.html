Understanding Carbon Footprints

A carbon footprint represents the total greenhouse gas emissions generated by daily activities such as transportation, electricity consumption, food production, and waste generation. Understanding these impacts helps individuals and organizations make environmentally responsible decisions.

Major Sources of Emissions

🚗 Transportation

Cars, flights, and fuel-based transport systems contribute significantly to global carbon emissions.

⚡ Electricity

Energy generated from fossil fuels remains one of the largest contributors to greenhouse gases.

🍔 Food Choices

Food production, especially meat-based diets, requires substantial energy and resources.

🗑 Waste

Improper waste disposal releases methane and other harmful greenhouse gases.
