About Our Project

Meet the team members, explore our vision, and understand our purpose in building this climate awareness platform.

Our Vision & Purpose

We live in a world where every action leaves an environmental trace, yet the carbon impact of daily habits remains invisible to most. Carbon Decode transforms hidden footprints into visible, manageable insights. Our purpose is to catalyze local action in schools, universities, and homes to foster collective, global change.

Why Carbon Tracking Matters

Carbon emissions contribute significantly to greenhouse effects, global warming, and environmental degradation. Carbon Decode empowers individuals and organizations with actionable data to measure their climate footprint, make sustainable adjustments, and build a cleaner, greener tomorrow.

Our Features

👤 Individual Calculator

Analyze personal lifestyle emissions, transport footprints, electricity choices, and environmental impacts to adjust daily behaviors.

🏢 Organization Calculator

Evaluate the environmental footprint of schools, colleges, or offices by tracking utilities, fleet transit, and waste levels.

📢 Climate Awareness

Explore comprehensive carbon resources, test your green knowledge with quizzes, and track live atmospheric indicators.
