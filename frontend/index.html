The Future Impact

🌡 Rising Temperatures

Global temperatures may rise by 2°C–3°C this century if greenhouse gas emissions continue unchecked.

🧊 Glacier Melting

Polar ice caps are melting at record rates, causing ocean levels to rise and threatening coastal cities.

🌊 Ocean Changes

Ocean warming and acidification disrupt marine ecosystems, collapse coral reefs, and threaten global food security.

Real-World Climate Action Highlights

🌳 Africa's Great Green Wall

An epic initiative growing an 8,000km natural wonder across the width of Africa, combating desertification, restoring 100 million hectares of degraded lands, and creating thousands of green jobs.

⚡ Global Renewable Surge

Solar and wind energy installations have officially surpassed fossil fuels in new power capacity additions worldwide, with solar drop-prices making it the cheapest utility in electricity history.

🇨🇷 Costa Rica's Forest Rebound

Through policy reforms and smart eco-taxation incentives, Costa Rica successfully doubled its tropical rainforest cover in the last three decades, going from 26% coverage to over 52% today.

🇪🇺 The European Green Deal

A legally-binding regional framework mapping Europe's complete transition to a climate-neutral continent by 2050, triggering billions in clean tech investments and grid transitions.
